Who we are

A German brand for Apple Watch bands and charms.

Wristneck is “The Charm Community”: bands, charms and accessories that let every customer style her Apple Watch like jewellery. We sell under our own brand in our own online shop and on EU marketplaces – starting in Germany, and we definitely plan to scale across all of Europe. Next to our current range, our own Wristneck collections are a clear focus for the future. We import into the EU under our own name and take full responsibility for product safety – that is why the EU documents matter so much to us.

Our standards

We only buy what we can legally sell in the EU.

For every item we need the following. Suppliers who can provide it get the order – at the same price, EU-ready always wins.

  • ✓
    Manufacturer data (GPSR)Factory name, full postal address and email for every product page.
  • ✓
    Nickel release EN 1811 / EN 12472For all metal parts with skin contact: buckles, connectors, chains, charms.
  • ✓
    Lead & cadmium (REACH)For metal parts, rhinestones, glass and crystal beads, enamel and paint.
  • ✓
    PAH / azo dyesPAH for silicone, TPU and rubber · azo dyes for nylon and fabric.
  • ✓
    Our importer labelWe send the text – it goes on every retail bag.
  • ✓
    Photos without logoOn a plain white background, with written permission to use them in our shop, on marketplaces and in ads.

How we work

Clear steps, long-term partners.

  1. QuotePrices at 100 / 500 / 1,000 pcs, MOQ per colour and size, lead time – in the Alibaba chat.
  2. SamplesOne sample per colour, ordered and paid via Alibaba Trade Assurance.
  3. Our checkWe photograph, measure and test every sample, incl. a nickel check, before we decide.
  4. Orders & reordersFirst orders of about 100–300 pcs per item, reorders of 500–1,000 pcs as the range grows.
